What is a Safe Space

A safe space is an environment intended to be free of bias, conflict, criticism, or potentially threatening actions, ideas, or conversations.

👥

Human presence

Accompanying peers and empathetic listeners who validate emotions and provide dependable support.

🤝

Congeniality

A friendly, warm, and welcoming atmosphere where cordiality and mutual respect thrive naturally.

🛡️

Humane protection

Safeguarding physical and emotional well-being, ensuring students feel secure from judgment or harm.
